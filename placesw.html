Tourist Places in Goa

1. Fort Aguada

A historic Portuguese-era fort offering beautiful sea views. It is a popular destination for history lovers and tourists.

2. Basilica of Bom Jesus

A famous historic church in Old Goa and a UNESCO World Heritage site, known for its beautiful architecture and history.

3. Dudhsagar Falls

A spectacular waterfall surrounded by greenery in the Western Ghats. It is known for its scenic natural beauty.

4. Fontainhas

A charming Latin Quarter in Panaji with colourful Portuguese-style houses, narrow streets and cultural heritage.
