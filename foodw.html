Famous Goan Food

1. Goan Fish Curry

A traditional coastal curry prepared with fresh fish, coconut milk and local spices. It is commonly enjoyed with steamed rice.

2. Fish Thali

A popular Goan meal served with rice, fish, curry, vegetables and delicious side dishes. It offers a taste of local cuisine.

3. Bebinca

A traditional layered Goan dessert made with ingredients such as coconut milk, eggs, sugar and flour.

4. Ros Omelette

A popular Goan street food featuring an omelette served with spicy, flavourful gravy, often accompanied by bread.
