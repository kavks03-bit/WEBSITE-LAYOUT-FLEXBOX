Famous Beaches in Goa

1. Baga Beach

Popular for beachside restaurants, water activities and lively surroundings.

2. Calangute Beach

A well-known beach with shopping, food, water sports and beautiful coastal views.

3. Palolem Beach

A scenic South Goa beach known for its curved coastline, palm trees and peaceful views.

4. Colva Beach

A popular South Goa destination for relaxing walks, golden sands and beautiful sunset views.
